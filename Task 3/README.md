# The Palm Room – A Luxurious Nigerian Restaurant App 🇳🇬✨

**Task 3 – UI/UX Design Project for CodSoft Internship**

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Welcome to **The Palm Room**, a digital dining experience that blends elegance, culture, and technology.

As a Nigerian, food is more than just a meal — it’s a story, a celebration, and a heritage. This design project brings that story to life through a luxurious mobile app that makes discovering and ordering rich, local cuisine effortless and visually delightful.

This is the third task in my **Product Design Internship at CodSoft**, and it's been one of the most exciting ones yet.

---

## 👩🏽‍🎨 My Design Story

Designing *The Palm Room* was about more than making an app — it was about crafting a **feeling**. From the first screen to the checkout experience, I focused on building a visual identity that feels:

- **Premium**, with bold black-yellow styling
- **Culturally rooted**, featuring local dishes and language
- **Effortless**, with intuitive user flows and neat hierarchy

---

## 📱 Key Screens

- **Splash Screen** – Welcomes you into the experience
- **Home Screen** – Showcases categories and featured dishes
- **Menu Listing** – Explore dishes filtered by type
- **Dish Details** – Ingredients, reviews, and extras like protein type
- **Cart** – Modify items and enter promo codes
- **Checkout** – Delivery/payment info and confirmation
- **Profile & Settings** – Manage account, view order history, and customize app experience
